Type an amount, a unit, `to` or `in`, and a target unit. A result row shows up
above your launchers, with no keyword needed:

```text
10 km to mi        → 10 km = 6.21371 mi
70f to c           → 70 °F = 21.1111 °C
5 GB in MB         → 5 GB = 5000 MB
3 cups to ml       → 3 cup = 709.765 ml
1.5 hours to min   → 1.5 h = 90 min
100 km/h to mph    → 100 km/h = 62.1371 mph
```

Enter copies the number and Ctrl+Enter copies it with its unit. The row
rounds to 6 significant digits; the copied value keeps 15, and whole numbers
are copied exactly.
Copying needs `wl-copy`, `xclip` or `xsel`.

<Aside type="note">
Unit conversion is on by default; see [Configuration](#configuration) to
turn it off.
</Aside>

Both units must measure the same thing, so `10 km to kg` shows nothing. A
target unit is required: `5 m` alone keeps searching your launchers. Math
expressions go to the [calculator](../calculator/) and currency
conversions to the [currency converter](../currency-converter/) first.

## Supported units

Unit names are case-insensitive, and most accept their full name and plural
too (`km`, `kilometer`, `kilometres`).

| Kind        | Units |
|-------------|-------|
| Length      | `mm`, `cm`, `m`, `km`, `in`, `ft`, `yd`, `mi`, `nmi` |
| Mass        | `mg`, `g`, `kg`, `t` (tonne), `oz`, `lb`, `st` (stone) |
| Volume      | `ml`, `cl`, `dl`, `l`, `cm3`/`cc`, `m3`, `tsp`, `tbsp`, `floz`/`fl oz`, `cup`, `pt`, `qt`, `gal` |
| Temperature | `c`, `f`, `k` (also `°C`, `celsius`, `fahrenheit`, `kelvin`) |
| Data        | `b`, `B`, `kb`, `kB`/`KB`, `Mb`, `MB`, `Gb`, `GB`, `Tb`, `TB`, `PB`, `Kib`, `KiB`, `Mib`, `MiB`, `Gib`, `GiB`, `TiB` |
| Time        | `ms`, `s`, `min`, `h`, `d`, `wk`, `yr` |
| Speed       | `m/s`, `km/h` (`kph`), `mph`, `kn` (knots), `ft/s` |
| Area        | `cm2`, `m2`, `km2`, `ha`, `ac`, `in2`, `ft2`, `yd2`, `mi2` (also `sq ft` and friends) |

Cups, pints, quarts, gallons and fluid ounces are US customary. Prefix pints,
quarts, gallons or fluid ounces with `imp` or `uk` for the imperial ones, as
in `2 uk gal to l`. A year is 365.25 days.

Data symbols are case-sensitive: a lowercase `b` means bits and an uppercase
`B` means bytes, so `Mb` is a megabit and `MB` a megabyte. `KB` and `kB` are
1000 bytes, while `KiB` is 1024. Spelled-out names such as `megabytes` work in
any case.

## Configuration

**`addons.units.enabled`** `boolean` (default: `true`)

Enable or disable unit conversion.

**`addons.units.close_on_action`** `boolean` (default: `true`)

Close Raffi after copying a converted value. Leave it unset to follow
`general.close_on_action`.

```yaml raffi.yaml
addons:
  units:
    enabled: false
```
